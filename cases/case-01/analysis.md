

# Case 01: Spinsy Welcome Pack

## Sender
 Spinsy Live Rewards <reese.harris@atyflashfortunepro.onmicrosoft.com>

## Subject
 phishing@pot, your $500 Spinsy Welcome Pack is ready

## What the email wants ?
The email wants the victim to click a link to claim the free $500 "Welcome Pack".
The link likely leads to a fake page that steals personal or payment information.
## Red flags 

- Too good to be true: it offers $500 for free to get a quick click.
- Sender domain: it comes from an onmicrosoft.com address, a generic Microsoft tenant domain, not a real Spinsy company domain.
- 
- Recipient: the To field says phishing@pot, a generic placeholder, not a real person's address.
- Urgency and a bait style subject: "your $500 Spinsy Welcome Pack is ready" pushes the reader to act without thinking.
